# Welcome to Loomark

Start typing. Everything is saved on this device automatically.

## Views

- **Text** for writing. What you type is what is saved.
- **Preview** for reading the formatted result.
- **Split** for both, side by side.

Switching views keeps your place.

## Focus Mode

The icon at the bottom right hides the controls and dims every paragraph but the one you are writing. Press it again, or press Escape, to exit.

## Anywhere

Sign in with Google from the `…` menu, then choose Sync on a document to write it on any device. Loomark also works offline, and you can export a `.md` file at any time.

The menu at the top left lists your documents. The pen at the top right starts a new one.
