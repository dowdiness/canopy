# Morning Pages

*Saturday, early. The kettle is still warm.*

Three pages before the day starts asking for things. No editing, no going back. If a sentence goes nowhere, let it go nowhere.

Woke before the alarm and lay listening to the rain thin out. The street below was the colour of pencil lead. Someone downstairs was already playing the same four bars of a song, again and again, the way you press a bruise to see if it still hurts.

I keep thinking about the woman at the station yesterday, reading a paperback with the cover folded back so no one could see the title. I want to know what it was. I want to write the version of her who never gets on the train.
